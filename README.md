A tiny stack virtual machine for the [make.fun competition tiny-stack-vm-01-run-20261122](https://make.fun/bounty/tiny-stack-vm-01-run-20261122) ($100 total, top 10 paid). `main.ts` (Node 22, pure stdlib) runs all 40 programs of a set: 32-bit wrapping integers, a 256-value stack, branches, calls, 256 words of memory, input/output, all ten traps with their exit codes, and the step and output limits.
